About Our Mission

For over 20 years, we've been fighting for children's rights

Who We Are

HelpTheChildren is a global nonprofit organization dedicated to ensuring every child has a healthy, educated, and safe future. Founded in 2003, we've grown into one of the world's leading children's charities, operating in over 120 countries.

Our History

What started as a small group of volunteers has become a global movement. Our founder, Sarah Johnson, saw the devastating impact of poverty on children in her community and decided to take action. Today, we've reached over 124 million children worldwide.

Our Approach

We believe in sustainable, community-led solutions. We work directly with local partners, families, and governments to create lasting change. Our programs focus on:

  • ✓ Emergency response and disaster relief
  • ✓ Healthcare and nutrition programs
  • ✓ Quality education for all children
  • ✓ Child protection and advocacy
